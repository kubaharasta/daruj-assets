Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • How can I delete my data?
    All data is stored exclusively and securely on your local device. You can delete it within the app settings using the data reset feature. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all saved records.
  • Why are the blood supply levels (Blood Barometer) not updating?
    The blood barometer fetches current data from the official websites of hospital transfusion departments. An active internet connection is required to download these updates. Some transfusion departments do not operate barometers or are not yet supported by the app.
  • Where can I find the official pre-donation questionnaire?
    The app includes a practice questionnaire for your own reference. The official and binding questionnaire must always be filled out physically or electronically directly at the transfusion center prior to donation.
