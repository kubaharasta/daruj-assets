1. Purpose and Nature of the Application

The Daruj mobile application is a comprehensive, non-commercial educational and motivational platform developed to support non-remunerated blood donation in the Czech Republic. Its goal is to digitalize and streamline donation-related processes while providing users with an independent personal journal.

Key features of the application include:

  • Smart Donation Journal: Clear tracking of completed donation history (whole blood, plasma, platelets, red blood cells) including automatic calculation of the next eligible donation date and detailed statistics.
  • Blood Barometer: Display of approximate blood supply levels for blood types across hospitals, provided via automated data collection from official sources.
  • Donation Guide: An integrated guide with a verified knowledge base reflecting the methodological materials of the Czech Red Cross and transfusion stations in the Czech Republic.
  • Gamification and Rewards: Tracking progress towards Czech Red Cross medals, collecting badges, and maintaining donor motivation.
  • Digital Questionnaire and Legislation: A practice eligibility questionnaire and a comprehensive overview of donor rights (paid leave, tax deductions) and health insurance benefits.
  • Donation Centers: A comprehensive directory of transfusion departments and donation centers including detailed information, opening hours, contacts, and the ability to select and manage your primary donation center for quick access.
IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: The outputs and recommendations of the application are for INFORMATIONAL and educational purposes only. The app is NOT a certified medical device. Its recommendations in no way REPLACE professional medical assessment, diagnosis, or the clinical decision of a physician at a transfusion department.

2. User Responsibility

By using the application, the user acknowledges and explicitly agrees to the following:

  • Informational Nature of Data: Calculations of future donation dates and guide answers serve as a tool for the donor's own orientation.
  • Final Eligibility Assessment: The final and legally/medically binding decision regarding a user's eligibility to donate blood or blood components is always made exclusively by a qualified physician at the transfusion department, based on an official questionnaire, physical examination, and laboratory screening immediately prior to donation.
