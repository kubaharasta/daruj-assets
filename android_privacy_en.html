Privacy Policy

App: Daruj (Android)

Author: Jakub Harašta

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The Daruj Android application is designed with a primary focus on user privacy and fully complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The app operates primarily offline as an independent personal blood and blood components donors journal.

1. Local Data Storage

All personal user data (donation history, profile settings, preferred donation center, and local cache) is stored exclusively in secure local device storage (via Room Database and Jetpack DataStore).

  • This data is never sent to any external servers or cloud storage.
  • Your personal records and health preferences are never used for advertising purposes, marketing, or provided to third parties.

2. Cloud Services and Network Data (Firebase)

The application uses Google Firebase exclusively for technical purposes and synchronization:

  • Firebase Remote Config: For fetching current configuration data, the educational knowledge base for the donation guide, and offline-first synchronization of donation centers and eligibility questionnaire.
  • Blood Barometer: Current blood supply levels are obtained through automated data collection (scraping) from publicly available official websites of hospital transfusion departments.

No personal identifiers or sensitive health data are sent to the cloud.

3. Complete Data Deletion

Users have full control over their data. The app settings include a data reset feature that immediately and permanently deletes all saved records, donation history, and settings, returning the app to its initial post-installation state.
